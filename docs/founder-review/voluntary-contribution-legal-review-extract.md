**Source:** `src/pages/project-clandestinus.astro`  
**Location:** “Book I — The First Gate” status note

> A future reward and verification framework is being developed but is not activated. Participation, eligibility, verification, funding and any future reward remain subject to official rules and activation notices.

**Source:** `src/pages/project-clandestinus.astro`  
**Location:** “Legal, financial and operational separation”, paragraph 1

> Project Clandestinus remains a separate commercial and creative intellectual-property venture. Its inclusion in Tree of Life does not transfer its ownership, intellectual property, publishing activities, finances, contracts, reward or prize obligations, or legal responsibilities to Visio Nova Mundi CIC.

**Source:** `src/pages/project-clandestinus.astro`  
**Location:** “Legal, financial and operational separation”, paragraph 2

> Likewise, grants, sponsorship or other funding received by Visio Nova Mundi CIC do not automatically finance Project Clandestinus. If the founder independently chooses to provide financial or in-kind support to the CIC and its public-benefit work, that support would be separately documented and subject to appropriate governance. It is not automatic, guaranteed or attached to every book sale.
