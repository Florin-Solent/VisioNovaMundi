# Reserved funding and prize wording — solicitor review brief

**Public website status:** The founder-funding narrative has been removed from the current founder-review build pending solicitor review. No current public page states or implies that a purchase, sale, entry, participation, revenue, profit or resource from an independent venture funds Visio Nova Mundi CIC, Unity House, Tree of Life or another CIC programme.

**Purpose:** This document records the current public boundary and the questions reserved for legal advice. It is not legal advice and does not authorise production publication.

## Current public wording

**Source:** `src/pages/impact.astro`
**Location:** “Two complementary pathways” → “Independent innovation”

> Founder-led ventures operate independently from Visio Nova Mundi CIC, with their own ownership, finances, intellectual property, contracts and legal responsibilities. They are not CIC programmes, and inclusion within the Tree of Life ecosystem does not create a financial or operational dependency between them and the CIC.

**Source:** `src/pages/project-clandestinus.astro`
**Location:** “Independent venture. Shared purpose.”

> Project Clandestinus remains independently owned and operated. Its inclusion within Tree of Life reflects its place within the founder's wider innovation ecosystem; it does not create a financial, legal or operational dependency between Project Clandestinus and Visio Nova Mundi CIC.

**Source:** `src/pages/project-clandestinus.astro`
**Location:** “Legal, financial and operational separation”

> Project Clandestinus remains a separate commercial and creative intellectual-property venture. Its inclusion in Tree of Life does not transfer its ownership, intellectual property, publishing activities, finances, contracts, reward or prize obligations, or legal responsibilities to Visio Nova Mundi CIC.

**Source:** `src/pages/project-clandestinus.astro`
**Location:** “Book I — The First Gate” status note

> A future reward and verification framework is being developed but is not activated. Participation, eligibility, verification, funding and any future reward remain subject to official rules and activation notices.

## Reserved solicitor questions

1. What wording, if any, could safely describe a future founder contribution without creating a consumer-facing funding promise or an implication that an independent-venture transaction benefits the CIC or a specific programme?
2. What company-law, CIC governance, accounting and asset-lock considerations apply if the founder later contributes money, resources or other support to the CIC?
3. What consumer, marketing and Gambling Act considerations apply to future Project Clandestinus reward, verification and prize communications, including any relationship between book sales, entry or participation and a future reward framework?
4. What documentation, approvals, disclosures and operational separation would be needed before any cross-promotion, data sharing or public funding statement?
